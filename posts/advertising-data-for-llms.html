Advertising data for LLMs

July 8, 2024

The business is selling ads, but the product is not a banner or sponsored search slot. The customer buys ad topics, and the company generates the RLHF or SFT data required to bias model behavior toward those topics.

The model builders are then paid to use that data. The hard questions are measurement, disclosure, user trust, and whether this becomes useful preference shaping or just a new way to smuggle advertising into model outputs.
