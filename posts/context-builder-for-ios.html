Context builder for iOS

August 30, 2025

A context builder app for iPhone. The premise is simple: highlight any piece of text, select an image, save a link, or grab some other content, then add it to a universal personal context through the built-in share sheet.

The product should focus almost entirely on removing the barrier to capture. It should be as easy as possible to add something from any app or website on the phone. The first version does not need to prove every downstream use case. It needs to prove that building and maintaining the context is effortless enough to become a habit.

Later, the context can become useful in more structured ways: automatically creating buckets of related material, gathering lifestyle guidelines and advice, or collecting everything relevant to an upcoming trip or house purchase in one place.
